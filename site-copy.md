# Toolbox — site copy

One block per section, in page order. Each block: headline, line under it,
body, call to action, and the picture it needs. Layout and look are Luca's.

---

## 1 · Hero

**Headline:** The workspace for companies that build things one at a time.

**Line:** We are not a software company, but we do ship code. We are not an AI
company, but we do use AI. We think you should too.

**Body:** One record per build, from the first quote to delivery. Every piece
of work on it is a tool, built for you.

**CTA:** Submit a tool

**Picture:** a live build workspace, nodes on the plate. Real screen, customer
names hidden.

---

## 2 · The gap

**Headline:** The order reaches the factory as a name and a date.

**Body:** Sales configures, quotes and promises in one system. The factory
builds in another. The CRM does not know a bill of materials; the ERP does not
know a customer was ever spoken to. Everything between them lives in
spreadsheets and inboxes.

**Line:** Toolbox is the record that spans the gap.

**Picture:** CRM ←  gap  → ERP, with the build record laid across it.

---

## 3 · One record

**Headline:** Sales and production open the same build.

**Body:** Each person sees the tools their job allows, and nothing else. The
salesperson sees everything ever done for that customer. The shop floor sees
what it needs to build, and no more.

**Picture:** one build, two views — the sales workspace and the production
workspace side by side.

---

## 4 · The tools

**Headline:** Create. Analyse. Operate.

**Create —** configurations, quotes, specification documents, teasers, QR
cards, listing sheets.

**Analyse —** build audits, questions to the ERP, lead research, parts,
labour and marketing reports.

**Operate —** job cards, pick lists, parts lists, handover, commercial
invoices.

**Line:** A tool takes one thing and makes another. One that does not fit the
open record is not offered.

**Picture:** three columns of tool keys, or a node opening on a build.

---

## 5 · Built for you

**Headline:** We are not a software company, but we do ship code.

**Steps:** Submit → Assess → Build → Deploy → Deliver

1. **Submit** — the work, and what it should produce.
2. **Assess** — what it takes, what it makes, one or many, who sees it.
3. **Build** — against your systems and your documents.
4. **Deploy** — a new node in your workspace. Nothing else moves.
5. **Deliver** — your team runs it; the output stays on the record.

**Line:** Tell us the work. We build the tool, and ship it into your workspace.

**CTA:** Submit a tool

**Picture:** the five steps as a single line.

---

## 6 · AI, where it earns its place

**Headline:** We are not an AI company, but we do use AI.

**Body:** A model is used where it saves real time, and every output is kept
with who asked and when.

- **Configure with AI** — a car described in words, priced.
- **Lead research** — a briefing before the first call.
- **Ask** — the ERP, answered in a sentence from live records.
- **Audit** — a build checked against its own history.

**Line:** We think you should too.

**Picture:** a lead research briefing, or a configuration made from a
sentence.
