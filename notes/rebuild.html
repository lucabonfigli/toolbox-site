Rebuilt, not plugged in.

We apply AI, but not by bolting it onto your existing stack. We rebuild systems to fit your exact use case, in the way that best suits your processes, using the smallest and most efficient AI models to keep long-term costs down. As AI improves and software gets cheaper to build, your operating costs fall with it.

Your business probably has an inefficiency that looks like a problem AI could solve. It can, but not by plugging in a chatbot. It takes rebuilding the infrastructure so that it suits each and every process.

80% of AI tasks can be completed by small language models. The work is in isolating each process, and simplifying its path to execution down to only what is necessary and most efficient.

Anti-agentic.

This is why we call ourselves anti-agentic. An agent is an AI model applied to a process with the goal of completing a task. Our goal is to isolate the process and optimise it, so that as little AI as possible is used to complete the task. The implementation is driven by the process, not by the model.

This does more than cut token cost, though it does that too: agents waste, on average, 15–30% of their token spend navigating poor processes. It also drastically reduces the cost of hallucination, the risk of an agent making an irreversible mistake.

We believe, and know, that LLMs, the essence of what modern AI is, are not sentient robotic beings. They are a great deal of maths used to make a best guess at the next word or outcome. Your business should not consistently depend on a best guess, even when that guess is very good and talks to you in a very nice tone.
